Elle Cold Brew

A visual identity and packaging concept created around a polished, cohesive cold brew brand.

Branding Packaging Illustrator Typography

Project

Elle Cold Brew

Role

Brand Designer

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

Focus

Identity · Packaging · Layout

01

Building the brand.

The project started with creating a visual direction that could carry across an entire brand rather than a single graphic.

I explored mood, typography, color, logo ideas, and packaging together so each piece felt like it belonged to the same visual system.

02

The goal.

Create a cohesive cold brew identity that feels intentional from the first impression to the packaging itself.

I wanted the brand to feel polished without becoming overly complicated. The focus was on consistency, hierarchy, readable typography, and visual balance.

What I did

My contributions

  • Built a moodboard to establish the overall brand tone and visual direction.
  • Explored logo concepts and refined them into a consistent identity.
  • Designed a can label using clear visual hierarchy and readable typography.
  • Iterated on spacing, type, and composition to create a more balanced final design.

What I learned

Skills gained

  • Creating hierarchy through typography and layout.
  • Turning a moodboard into a cohesive visual identity.
  • Building and refining assets in Adobe Illustrator.
  • Using iteration and critique to improve the final design.
