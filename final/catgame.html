01

About the game.

The player moves through the world as a cat, completing challenges and exploring different parts of a day in feline life.

The project combines programming with storytelling. Players make choices, complete mini-games, interact with objects, and work through different challenges as they progress.

02

The goal.

We wanted to make a game that felt playful, interactive, and easy to explore.

The project gave me the chance to practice Python while thinking about how player choices, visual elements, and small interactions can make a digital experience feel more engaging.

What I did

My contributions

  • Helped design the storyline, character interactions, and decision paths.
  • Developed gameplay mechanics including movement, collecting items, and challenges.
  • Used Python functions to organize game events and player choices.
  • Tested edge cases and debugged the game to keep progression working smoothly.

What I learned

Skills gained

  • Python functions, variables, loops, and conditionals.
  • Designing interactive experiences around player choices.
  • Breaking a larger game into smaller reusable pieces.
  • Working collaboratively on a creative technical project.
