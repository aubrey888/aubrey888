Hay Day Study

Exploring how community, social systems, and player identity influence engagement in a mobile game.

Research UX Analysis Presentation

Project

Hay Day Digital Culture Study

Role

Researcher

Method

Research · Synthesis

Focus

Community · Retention · UX

01

Looking beyond the gameplay.

I studied Hay Day as more than a mobile game — I looked at it as a social environment.

The project explored how social features, community dynamics, player demographics, and identity can shape the way people interact with a digital product over time.

02

The research question.

What makes players continue returning to a game, even after the novelty of the gameplay itself begins to wear off?

I focused on the relationship between community features and retention, including how social interaction can give users a stronger sense of identity and belonging inside a digital product.

What I did

My contributions

  • Researched player demographics, community behavior, and engagement.
  • Looked at how social perception and community influence player retention.
  • Synthesized research into a clear presentation and narrative.
  • Connected findings back to UX and product design considerations.

What I learned

Skills gained

  • Research synthesis and identifying meaningful patterns.
  • Thinking about engagement and retention from a UX perspective.
  • Turning information into a clear visual story.
  • Presenting findings in a way that is easy for others to understand.
