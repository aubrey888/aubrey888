Vote Animation

A hand-drawn animation created in Procreate to encourage people to participate and make their voices heard.

Art · Motion · Messaging

01

Creating a message through motion.

This project was about using illustration and animation to communicate an idea quickly.

I created the animation in Procreate for an image-based class project. The goal was to make something visually engaging that encourages people to vote and take an active role in their community.

02

The animation.

The final piece uses hand-drawn frames and movement to turn a simple visual idea into something more expressive.

Creative process

What I focused on

  • Communicating a clear message without relying on a lot of text.
  • Using hand-drawn frames to create understandable motion.
  • Thinking about pacing and visual rhythm.
  • Creating a piece that feels expressive while still serving a purpose.

What I learned

Takeaways

  • Animation can change how a viewer experiences a simple visual message.
  • Small changes between frames can dramatically affect pacing.
  • Simplicity can make communication more memorable.
  • Illustration and motion are useful tools for digital storytelling.
