About me

Curious by nature.
Creative by design.

I'm an Information Science student at the University of Colorado Boulder interested in creating experiences that feel intuitive, beautiful, and meaningful.

My work crosses UX design, research, development, interactive media, and visual branding. I enjoy understanding why people behave the way they do, finding patterns in information, and transforming those insights into thoughtful experiences.

I'm especially drawn to work where design and analysis meet — from refining a digital customer experience to studying the behavior behind it.
